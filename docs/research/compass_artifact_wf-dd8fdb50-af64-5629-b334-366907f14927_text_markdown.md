# ZISUN V2 Pressure-Test & Launch Plan — Second-Round Deep Research Brief

## TL;DR
- **The V2 P0/30-day technical sequencing is sound and should ship as designed, with three corrections:** shorten the pincode Redis cache to ~6h for the COD-availability flag (which changes intraday) while keeping fail-open; set the free-shipping threshold at **₹1,999, not ₹2,499** (₹2,499 sits at the top of your own AOV range and is unreachable for most single-item carts, killing the goal-gradient effect); and build abandoned-cart **Message 1 as a Utility template** to cut per-message cost ~7-8x.
- **On the biggest open question — marketplaces — do NOT launch on Meesho at all** (its buyer base tops out well below ₹1,999 and ethnic-wear return rates run 18-32%), and **delay Myntra/Amazon until you have 90 days of D2C data.** A ₹1,999 kurti is structurally unsellable to Meesho's sub-₹500 audience; manual stock allocation from a dedicated per-channel pool is fine below ~500 orders/month, so no inventory-sync middleware is warranted yet.
- **The real gap is go-to-market: budget ~₹1.5–3L and 8–12 weeks to your first 100 orders**, led by micro-influencer barter/seeding + founder network + organic Reels, with Meta ads held back until you have 8-12 proven organic creatives, and the launch timed to land inventory before the Sept–Nov festive/Diwali peak (Diwali 2026 = Sunday 8 November, also a Tamil Nadu public holiday).

## Key Findings

### 1. Shiprocket & logistics
- **The serviceability API returns exactly what the product page needs.** Shiprocket's `courier/serviceability` endpoint takes `pickup_postcode`, `delivery_postcode`, `weight`, and `cod` (0/1), and returns a list of available courier companies with per-courier cost, **estimated delivery days**, and courier details. COD availability is returned per courier and depends on courier coverage and account eligibility. One call gives you both the serviceability check and an EDD basis.
- **The 24h Redis cache is too long for the COD flag; fail-open is correct.** Courier serviceability — and especially per-pincode COD eligibility — changes intraday (couriers pause COD to specific pincodes on RTO spikes or local disruptions); prepaid serviceability is far more stable. Cache prepaid serviceability 24h but the COD flag only ~6h, and always **fail-open** on API timeout: a false "not serviceable" kills a real order, whereas a false "serviceable" only risks one RTO you can catch at the COD-confirmation step.
- **EDD: display one padded date.** Take the recommended courier's estimated delivery days, add a 1-day dispatch buffer, and render a single date ("Delivery by Tue, 25 Aug"). Shiprocket EDD is a courier-provided estimate, not a guarantee — under-promise.
- **COD remittance is D+8 working days standard (~10 calendar days to bank); Early COD offers D+2 for a fee.** At ~50% COD you continuously finance ~10 days of COD GMV — model this working-capital drag explicitly.
- **Weight-discrepancy disputes are Shiprocket's most-cited operational problem.** Independent reviews (Capterra, Shopify app store, Trustpilot summaries) repeatedly report couriers billing a 200g packet as 1.5kg, wallet debits, and a dispute process that takes a minimum of 7 days with the wallet amount held during review. Mitigation: enter accurate dimensions/weight, keep packed-weight photos, and reconcile the weight-discrepancy report monthly.
- **Alternatives for a small TN brand:** Delhivery Direct, iThink Logistics, NimbusPost, and Pickrr compete on similar aggregator models. Below 500 orders/month, stay on Shiprocket (no monthly fee, no minimum commitment) for convenience; only pilot a second courier/aggregator on a slice of volume once you cross ~1,000/month, comparing RTO and delivery-speed for a full month before migrating.
- **Shiprocket's COD-verification/RTO add-ons vs your own WhatsApp flow:** you already run a WhatsApp transactional pipeline in production, so build COD confirmation on your own stack. An add-on duplicates capability you own and adds per-order cost.

### 2. WhatsApp Business API — costs & compliance India 2025-2026
- **Pricing shifted from per-conversation to per-message on July 1, 2025.** India Meta per-message rates (before BSP markup): **Marketing ₹0.8631** (raised 10.0% on 1 Jan 2026 from ₹0.7846, announced with the standard 60-day notice in early November 2025 — RichAutomate citing Meta's rate card); **Utility/Authentication held at ~₹0.115** (Whautomate India rate card, Jul 2026). Service messages (free-form replies within a customer-initiated 24h window) are free, and utility templates sent inside an open service window are also free.
- **Monthly cost modeling (Meta fees only, before BSP platform fee):**
  - *100 orders/mo:* ~350 utility msgs (~₹40) + ~300 marketing abandoned-cart msgs (~₹260) ≈ **₹300-400/mo.**
  - *500 orders/mo:* ~1,750 utility (~₹200) + ~1,500 marketing (~₹1,300) ≈ **₹1,500-1,700/mo.**
  - *1,000 orders/mo:* ~3,500 utility (~₹450) + ~3,000 marketing (~₹2,600) ≈ **₹3,000-3,200/mo.**
  - Marketing (abandoned-cart) messages dominate at ~7-8x utility. Keeping abandoned-cart Message 1 as a Utility-classified reminder (no discount, no promo language) is the single biggest cost lever.
- **BSP comparison (platform fee on top of Meta fees):** AiSensy is cheapest/most transparent (plans from ~₹999-1,500/mo, transparent Meta pass-through at ₹1.09 marketing / ₹0.145 utility). Interakt has the deepest native Shopify/e-commerce integration but bills active-contact tiers. Wati's markup is least transparent (independent analysts report 20%-257% depending on category) and it charges extra for an account manager. For a custom FastAPI stack you don't need deep platform features — you need clean API access and low markup, which points to **AiSensy or the direct Meta Cloud API**. Gupshup/MSG91 are viable but pricier/enterprise-leaning.
- **New WABA number scaling (Meta for Developers docs, confirmed):** a new business portfolio starts at **250 unique customers/24h**; this rises to **2,000** by either completing Meta Business Verification OR sending 2,000 delivered messages to unique users within a 30-day moving window using high-quality templates. Auto-scaling to **10,000 → 100,000 → unlimited** then requires (a) Medium/High quality across all numbers/templates and (b) using ≥50% of your current limit in the trailing 7 days; Meta re-evaluates every 6 hours. Since Oct 7, 2025 limits are portfolio-level (shared across all numbers), so adding numbers does NOT add capacity. Quality is green/yellow/red on a rolling 7-day window; blocks, spam reports, and low engagement lower it. Per newer Meta-aligned sources the old "Flagged→auto-downgrade after 7 days" status was removed in 2025-2026 — a Red rating now freezes upgrades rather than cutting your limit (older BSP blogs still claim auto-downgrade; contested).
- **Template approval:** most templates auto-approve in 1-30 minutes; Meta allows up to 24-48h for human review. COD-confirmation = **Utility**; abandoned-cart = **Marketing** (not Utility). Since **April 9, 2025** Meta validates category at review and will re-classify a Utility template to Marketing — or reject it with reason `INCORRECT_CATEGORY` — if it contains promotional content. Rejection/reclassification triggers: promo words ("offer", "discount", "shop now"), emojis and "friendly" tone in a utility template, URL shorteners/wa.me links, a placeholder `{{1}}` at the very start or end of the message (auto-reject), and missing opt-out on marketing templates. Rule: write COD confirmation "like a receipt, not like a friend."
- **DPDP Act 2023 + DPDP Rules 2025 (compliance status):** MeitY notified the Rules on **13 Nov 2025**; three-phase rollout — Phase 1 (Data Protection Board operational) 13 Nov 2025, Phase 2 (consent-manager registration) 13 Nov 2026, Phase 3 (full substantive consent/notice/rights compliance) **13 May 2027**. Consent must be "free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, given by clear affirmative action" (Section 6(1)) and withdrawal must be as easy as giving it (Section 6(4)). Transactional COD/order messages rest on a separate lawful basis (voluntary provision, Section 7) and do NOT need a marketing opt-in. **Abandoned-cart messages are Marketing and DO require a documented, timestamped opt-in.** Max penalty ₹250 crore (for failing to implement reasonable security safeguards); ₹200 crore for breach-notification failure (Schedule 1). "They messaged us first" is not a valid consent record. A compliant checkout opt-in = an **unticked** checkbox ("Get order updates and offers on WhatsApp"), a stored timestamped consent event, and an easy STOP/opt-out path.
- **Abandoned-cart sequence (best-practice consensus):** 3 messages over ~24-48h — Message 1 at T+15-30min (plain reminder, no discount, keep Utility-classified), Message 2 at T+6-12h (objection handling / social proof), Message 3 at T+24-30h (time-boxed incentive, Marketing). Never exceed 3; stop instantly on purchase; only discount in Message 3 to avoid training deliberate abandonment. WhatsApp cart-recovery conversion runs materially higher than email in vendor case data (15-30% vs 2-5% email; treat exact figures as vendor marketing).
- **COD confirmation:** use interactive quick-reply buttons (Confirm / Cancel) — higher response than free-text. Confirm within minutes (GoKwik first-party data: response rates drop ~40% after 30 minutes); auto-hold/cancel unconfirmed COD orders after 2-4h with one reminder.

### 3. Marketplace decision — the biggest strategic question
- **Meesho: skip it at ZISUN's price point.** Meesho is genuinely 0% commission but the buyer base is sub-₹500 (best-sellers cluster ₹150-600; ethnic-wear volume dominated by kurtis under ₹300). Its real cost stack is ~15-22% of price (shipping ~₹65/parcel, ₹25-30 fixed fee, 18% GST on fees, reverse-shipping ₹150-165 on returns), and women's ethnic-wear return rates run **18-32%**. A ₹1,999 kurti would be the most expensive listing in a price-first market. Payment cycle ~7-15 days after delivery.
- **Myntra: the right eventual home, but gated.** Brand-tier / brand-approval gated; new brands enter at the **highest commission band (15-30% for premium fashion)** and negotiate down as brand-tier credit accrues, plus closing/collection/shipping fees and 18% GST on fees — net take-home typically **18-35% below GMV**. Fits AOV >₹999 brand-led identities but requires brand approval and 3:4 (1500×2000) catalogue imagery.
- **Amazon (~9% referral for apparel) / Flipkart (~8%)** are more accessible than Myntra and better for margin/brand than Meesho, but they split scarce founder focus while you're still finding creative-market fit.
- **Inventory sync:** middleware (Unicommerce, EasyEcom, Vinculum) is priced for scale — advanced tiers start ~₹45,000/mo and only pay off above ~₹50L GMV or ~5,000 orders/mo. Below ~500 orders/mo, **manual stock allocation from a dedicated per-channel pool is the correct, standard approach** — do not buy middleware. Zoho Inventory (cheap, GST-native) is the low-cost bridge if you later run two channels before you're ready for Unicommerce.
- **Verdict with numbers:** with a finished D2C site and an Instagram presence being built, launching marketplaces simultaneously would (a) dilute the founder attention that first-100-orders GTM requires, (b) expose you to an 18-35% marketplace take plus higher fashion return rates before you know your own return/RTO baseline, and (c) buy inventory-sync complexity you don't need. **Sequence: D2C-only for 90 days → then Myntra brand application + Amazon as a margin channel once you know your contribution margin and size-sellthrough. Only pull marketplaces forward if D2C CAC proves structurally unviable** (blended CAC persistently above per-order contribution margin after creative iteration).

### 4. Launch / first-100-orders GTM (the documented gap)
- **Channel mix that works for kurti/co-ord brands:**
  - *Micro-influencer seeding (10k-100k)* is the highest-ROI first move. Rates: nano (1k-10k) barter–₹8k; micro (10k-100k) ₹8k-80k per Reel, with fashion micros at ~50k charging ₹25-60k. Barter (free product = COGS + shipping) is standard for nano/micro seeding. Run 8-12 micro creators for content variety rather than one macro. Track cost per acquired customer, not cost per post.
  - *Organic Instagram Reels* are the default discovery engine for sub-₹2,000 lifestyle products; authentic mobile-shot content outperforms polished studio work. Post consistently and boost the top organic Reels (≥2x your average engagement) into paid.
  - *Meta ads:* minimum viable test budget ~₹500/day (₹15,000/mo); below this the algorithm can't exit the learning phase (~50 conversions/ad set/7 days). Fashion CPMs ₹80-150 (some cite ₹160-240), CPC ₹5-15, new-brand CAC ₹400-800, and **realistic new-brand ROAS 2.0-2.8x on a 7-day window — which becomes ~1.9-2.1x after 18-28% fashion returns**. Break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ gross margin. Don't scale paid until organic creatives already convert.
  - *WhatsApp broadcast/community + founder network* delivers the actual first orders (network + one or two posts that travel).
  - *Offline leverage:* exhibitions/pop-ups are a proven low-CAC channel for Tamil Nadu apparel brands and double as content/photography opportunities.
- **Budget & timeline to first 100 orders: ~₹1.5-3L over 8-12 weeks** — dominated by seeding product cost + a small paid test + photography, NOT big ad spend.
- **Launch offers:** first-order free shipping or a small gift beats a steep first-order discount, which trains discount-dependence. If you use a code, keep it modest and time-boxed, and lean on your prepaid-incentive coupon (already built) to shift COD→prepaid rather than as a blanket discount.

### 5. Validation of specific V2 technical choices
- **Fail-open on pincode outage: correct.** Established Indian checkouts fail-open — a serviceability timeout should never block an otherwise-valid order; catch bad addresses at the COD-confirmation step. Fail-closed would convert an infra blip into lost revenue.
- **Cart-before-login with OTP: correct direction, but push the OTP wall as late as possible.** Baymard's 2024/2025 checkout study (n≈4,384 US adults) found mandatory account creation cited by ~24-26% of abandoners (surprise "extra costs" is the #1 fixable reason at 39%); removing the forced-account requirement lifts checkout completion 15-35%. Indian D2C checkout completion benchmarks are 35-55%; below 30% usually means forced account/slow OTP. Best pattern: full guest-style cart and checkout, OTP at the payment step (India norm — and your OTP doubles as phone verification for COD-confirmation and cart-recovery consent), with cart carry-over across OTP login as planned. Do NOT put the OTP wall before the cart.
- **Free-shipping threshold: lower to ₹1,999.** The rule is 15-30% above AOV. With AOV ₹1,499-2,499, ₹2,499 sits AT the top of the range — most single-item carts (₹1,499-1,999) can't reach it without doubling the order, so the goal-gradient effect dies. ₹1,999 is ~15-30% above the low end and reachable with one add-on for a ₹1,499 buyer. Target 50-65% of orders qualifying. Implement a dynamic progress bar ("You're ₹300 away from free shipping") — progress bars add 12-18% AOV vs static messaging.
- **COD confirmation timing: within minutes, button-reply.** Confirm within 5 minutes (response drops ~40% after 30 min per GoKwik); use Confirm/Cancel quick-reply buttons, not open-ended text.
- **GA4 vs your own analytics_events table:** GA4 alone cannot compute your four survival metrics (contribution margin by payment method, 90/180-day repeat rate, funnel-to-delivered, size-level sellthrough) — it lacks cost/COD-remittance/RTO data and its sampling limits cohort accuracy. Point **Metabase at a Postgres read replica** and build these four as SQL questions off `analytics_events` + orders + shipments. Metabase is open-source (AGPL, self-host on Docker), no per-seat fee, native Postgres connector. Keep GA4 for top-of-funnel acquisition attribution only.

### 6. Blind spots both documents still miss
- **GST rate change (major, effective 22 Sept 2025):** The 12% apparel slab was abolished. Per the Ministry of Finance's Lok Sabha reply (56th GST Council): garments with sale value ≤₹2,500/piece = **5% GST**; >₹2,500 = **18% GST** (up from 12%), threshold raised from the old ₹1,000. Everything in ZISUN's ₹1,499-2,499 band attracts only **5% GST** — a margin tailwind vs the old 12%. The threshold is on assessable value (Sec 15 CGST Act), and there is a hard pricing cliff at ₹2,500: a product at ₹2,600 jumps to 18%. Respect the cliff when pricing.
- **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules:** e-commerce product pages must display, before purchase, mandatory declarations next to the product — manufacturer/packer name & address, country of origin, generic commodity name, net quantity, MRP inclusive of all taxes, consumer-care contact, and **dimensions** (explicitly required for apparel). Frequently missed by D2C sites and enforceable; add these fields to the PDP template.
- **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020:** mandatory clear return/refund/exchange policy; a named Grievance Officer with contact displayed (acknowledge complaints in 48h, resolve in ~1 month); total price with all taxes/fees shown before checkout; no cancellation charge on pre-shipment cancellation; and the 2023 dark-pattern ban (already respected). Refunds for defective/not-as-described goods must go to the original payment method (RBI: to source within 5-7 working days); store credit cannot be forced for defective goods.
- **Returns operations:** for defective/wrong items the brand pays return shipping and owes refund-to-source; for change-of-mind/fit returns you may set policy (store credit/exchange is legal for non-defective returns if clearly stated up front). Fashion return rates run 25-40% — bake this into unit economics.
- **Packaging/unboxing** is a cheap retention lever for a ₹1,499-2,499 brand — branded mailer + thank-you card + size/care insert lifts repeat and UGC — but keep packed weight down to avoid weight-discrepancy charges.
- **Product photography on a budget:** model shoots beat AI-generated imagery for ethnic fit/drape credibility and trust; combine a low-cost local model shoot with influencer-seeded UGC and exhibition content rather than AR try-on (correctly de-scoped).
- **Customer-service load:** Indian buyers expect fast WhatsApp replies; set a response-time SLA (e.g., first response <2h in working hours) since WhatsApp is your primary channel and slow replies hurt both conversion and quality rating.
- **Festive calendar (critical for a late-2026 launch):** Diwali 2026 is Sunday **8 November** (Lakshmi Puja; five-day festival Fri 6 Nov–Tue 10 Nov; also a Tamil Nadu public holiday), with festive shopping starting 3-4 weeks earlier — inventory and campaigns must be live by early-mid October. Sharad Navratri/Dussehra peak mid-late October. **Pongal (mid-Jan)** is the key Tamil Nadu regional peak (~30-40% apparel uplift locally), and January CPMs are near annual lows (good creative-testing window). Wedding season spans Nov-Feb. Sept-Nov is 40-55% of annual revenue for many Indian fashion brands — build audience through Aug-Sept and convert into the Oct-Nov peak.

## Recommendations (staged, with thresholds)

**P0 (Days 1-4) — ship as planned, with these corrections:**
1. COD WhatsApp confirmation: Utility template, Confirm/Cancel buttons, fire within 5 min, auto-hold unconfirmed after ~3h with one reminder.
2. Prepaid-only coupon: keep as the primary COD→prepaid lever (prepaid returns <2% vs COD ~26%).
3. cm size charts as modal + trust badges beside Add-to-Cart: ship as planned.
4. Real pincode check via Shiprocket: cache prepaid serviceability 24h, COD flag ~6h, **fail-open** on timeout; render EDD as one padded date.
5. Add Legal Metrology declarations + Grievance Officer + total-price-before-checkout to templates now (compliance, near-zero cost).

**30 days:**
6. Photo-review upload UI (JSONB column already exists).
7. Cart-before-login with OTP at the payment step; cart carry-over across login.
8. Abandoned-cart 3-message sequence with a **timestamped checkout opt-in checkbox** (DPDP); Message 1 Utility, Message 3 Marketing.
9. Free-shipping threshold **₹1,999** with dynamic progress bar; monitor qualification rate toward 50-65%.

**Next quarter:**
10. Metabase on a Postgres read replica for the four survival metrics.
11. Shiprocket inbound webhook for funnel-to-delivered + RTO tracking.
12. Marketplace decision: apply to Myntra + open Amazon **only after 90 days of D2C contribution-margin and size-sellthrough data**. Skip Meesho.

**GTM (parallel):** micro-influencer barter seeding + organic Reels first; hold Meta ads until 8-12 converting organic creatives exist; time inventory + campaign ramp to the Oct-Nov festive peak.

**Thresholds that change the plan:** if blended CAC stays above per-order contribution margin after creative iteration → pause paid, lean on organic/seeding + marketplaces for volume. If COD RTO stays >25% after the confirmation flow → tighten to prepaid-only for flagged pincodes. If checkout completion <30% → the OTP/account wall is misplaced.

## Revised 8-Week Sequencing Plan (V2 technical + GTM merged)

**Week 1 — Foundation & compliance.** Ship P0 items 1-5 (COD WhatsApp confirmation with Confirm/Cancel buttons + 5-min fire; prepaid-only coupon; cm size modal; trust badges; real pincode check with 6h COD-cache + fail-open). Add Legal Metrology declarations, Grievance Officer, and all-in pricing to PDP/checkout. Register WABA number, complete Meta Business Verification (jumps limit 250→2,000), and submit COD-confirmation (Utility) template for approval. Lock festive inventory decisions (production lead times demand it now for an Oct-Nov peak).

**Week 2 — Content engine + seeding kickoff.** Low-cost local model shoot (hero product photography). Draft 8-12 organic Reel concepts. Identify and DM 15-20 micro/nano fashion influencers in Tamil Nadu + pan-India for barter seeding; ship product. Set up WhatsApp broadcast opt-in and founder-network soft launch.

**Week 3 — Soft launch to warm audience.** Publish first Reels cadence (3-4/week). Convert founder network + waitlist via WhatsApp. First seeded influencer posts go live. Submit abandoned-cart templates (Message 1 Utility, Message 3 Marketing) for approval. Target: first 10-20 orders from warm traffic.

**Week 4 — Build the review flywheel.** Ship photo-review upload UI; solicit reviews from first buyers. Continue Reels + second seeding wave. Instrument analytics: stand up Metabase on read replica; start tracking funnel-to-delivered and COD RTO. Target: cumulative 25-35 orders.

**Week 5 — Checkout optimization live.** Deploy cart-before-login with OTP at payment step + cart carry-over. Launch ₹1,999 free-shipping threshold with progress bar. Turn on the 3-message abandoned-cart sequence (with timestamped opt-in). Target: measurable lift in checkout completion toward 40%+.

**Week 6 — First paid test.** With 8-12 organic creatives now proven, start Meta ads at ₹500-750/day: broad prospecting + retargeting cart abandoners. Measure CAC vs contribution margin and net ROAS after returns. Keep seeding running. Target: cumulative 50-60 orders.

**Week 7 — Scale what works.** Duplicate winning ad sets (20-25%/day budget increases); feed best organic Reels into paid. Third seeding wave timed to early-October festive search ramp. Begin festive collection teasers. Target: cumulative 75-85 orders.

**Week 8 — Festive ramp + marketplace prep.** Full festive campaign live (inventory + creative ready by early-mid October for Diwali 8 Nov). Hit/exceed 100 orders. Begin compiling the 90-day D2C dataset (contribution margin by payment method, size-sellthrough, RTO) that will drive the Myntra/Amazon go/no-go decision. Do NOT touch Meesho.

## Caveats
- WhatsApp RTO-reduction percentages (20-40%) are almost entirely vendor marketing and uncited; base RTO rates are contested (21%-58% within one Unicommerce dataset). The only source-backed figure is timing (GoKwik: response drops ~40% after 30 min). Treat any "cut RTO by X%" claim skeptically.
- WhatsApp cart-recovery conversion figures (15-30%) come from BSP/tool blogs, not independent studies.
- Meta and BSP per-message rates change (India marketing rose 10% on 1 Jan 2026 to ₹0.8631); verify current rates in the BSP portal before finalizing budgets.
- Free-shipping-threshold and Meta-ads benchmark ranges (CPM, ROAS, CAC) are drawn from agency/vendor blogs and are orientation, not guarantees — validate against your own data within 30 days.
- DPDP full enforcement (13 May 2027) and consent-manager registration (13 Nov 2026) are per the notified Rules, but implementation guidance is still evolving.
- Shiprocket EDD is a courier-provided estimate, not a guarantee; pad it.
