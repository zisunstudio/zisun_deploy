1. Product Overview

1.1 Vision
ZISUN is a mobile-first, content-driven commerce platform that fuses storytelling with shopping. Unlike traditional eCommerce catalogues, ZISUN puts discovery at the center of the experience — every piece of content is a potential transaction, and every transaction is a story.

1.2 Problem Statement
Indian eCommerce today suffers from three compounding failures:
Discovery deficit: Search-first commerce assumes users know what they want. Most users — especially in fashion — do not. They need inspiration, context, and narrative.
Trust gap: Generic product listings with stock photos and templated descriptions do not build brand affinity. Conversion rates remain low because emotional connection is absent.
Experience fragmentation: Content lives on Instagram and YouTube; purchasing happens on Flipkart or Myntra. There is no single platform that closes the loop from story to sale in one gesture.

1.3 Solution Overview
ZISUN closes the gap between content consumption and commerce execution:
Shoppable content: Every reel, story card, and editorial is directly linked to purchasable products. The content IS the storefront.
Mobile-native checkout: Frictionless OTP auth, one-tap payment via Razorpay, and WhatsApp-based order management for users who prefer messaging over apps.
Story-led brand building: Vendors present products through curated narratives — occasion-based, season-based, lifestyle-based — not SKU grids.
AI personalization (Phase 2+): Behavioral signals feed a recommendation engine that adapts the discovery feed to each user's style fingerprint.

4. Core Features

4.1 OTP-Based Authentication
Priority  P0 — Required for all commerce activity

Description
Phone number-based login using OTP (SMS/WhatsApp delivery). No passwords. JWT issued on successful verification with refresh token rotation.
User Story
As a first-time user, I want to sign in with my phone number so that I don't need to create or remember a password.
Acceptance Criteria
User enters 10-digit Indian mobile number; system validates format before sending OTP.
OTP is 6 digits, valid for 5 minutes, delivered within 10 seconds via SMS.
Maximum 5 OTP requests per phone per hour; after limit, user sees a cooldown message.
Successful OTP returns access token (15-min expiry) + refresh token (30-day expiry, stored httpOnly).
Refresh token rotation: every refresh generates a new refresh token and invalidates the old one.
Admin role users require a secondary confirmation step (admin-specific OTP flag in DB).

Priority
Acceptance Criteria
Edge Cases
P0
OTP expires after 5 min; max 5 attempts before lockout; SMS fallback if WhatsApp fails
SIM swap attack mitigation; concurrent login from two devices; invalid number edge

4.2 Shoppable Content Feed
Priority  P0 — Core product differentiator

Description
A vertically scrollable, story-first feed where each card links to shoppable products. Content types: editorial images, short video reels, occasion bundles, product spotlights.
User Story
As a fashion-curious user, I want to scroll a curated feed and tap any item to instantly view and purchase it, so I can shop without actively searching.
Acceptance Criteria
Feed loads within 2 seconds on 4G with skeleton loading states.
Each content card displays: media (image or video thumbnail), caption, and a "Shop Now" CTA with price.
Tapping a product within content opens a bottom sheet (not a new page) with product details, size selection, and Add to Cart.
Video autoplay is muted by default; tap to unmute.
Feed is paginated (20 cards per page); infinite scroll with preloading next page at 80% scroll depth.
Content is tagged by: occasion, season, price band, category. Used for personalization.

4.3 Product Catalog & Inventory
Priority  P0

Description
Admin-managed product catalog with support for variants (size, color), media (images + video), category tagging, and real-time stock tracking.
User Story
As an admin, I want to create and manage products with variants so that customers always see accurate availability and pricing.
Acceptance Criteria
Product creation: name, description, base price, sale price, category, tags, images (min 1, max 10), video (optional).
Variants: each variant has its own SKU, stock count, and price delta.
Inventory decrement is atomic: uses SELECT FOR UPDATE or Redis DECR. Overselling is impossible.
Out-of-stock variants are visually disabled (not hidden) with "Notify Me" CTA.
Soft delete on products: deleted products remain in order history but are hidden from catalog.
Admin can bulk update stock via CSV import.

4.4 Cart & Checkout
Priority  P0

Description
A streamlined checkout flow: cart review → address selection → payment method → order confirmation. Target: under 90 seconds from cart to confirmation.
Acceptance Criteria
Cart persists across sessions (stored server-side, linked to user ID).
Checkout step 1: cart review with item, quantity, price, and subtotal.
Checkout step 2: address selection (saved addresses) or new address entry with pincode-based auto-fill for city/state.
Checkout step 3: payment — Razorpay (card, UPI, netbanking, wallets) as primary; COD as secondary for eligible pincodes.
Order created in DB before payment initiated; status = PAYMENT_PENDING.
Payment success webhook processes idempotently using payment_gateway_id unique constraint.
Order confirmation sent via WhatsApp + in-app notification within 60 seconds of payment.

9. UX & Design Guidelines

9.1 Design Principles
Content first, commerce second: The product must feel like a magazine that sells, not a shop that tells stories. Every design decision must serve the content experience first.
Zero friction to purchase: Every additional tap is a potential abandonment. The checkout flow must be the shortest legally and technically possible.
Trust through clarity: Price, delivery estimate, and return policy visible on every product page — no hidden information that requires scrolling to find.
Accessible at entry-level: Design must work on a 5-inch, 720p screen on a mid-range Android. Premium UI is achievable without requiring premium hardware.

9.2 Mobile-First Considerations
Minimum tap target size: 44x44pt (iOS HIG standard).
Bottom sheet pattern for product quick-view (avoids full page navigation).
Thumb-zone optimized CTA placement: primary actions within natural thumb reach (bottom 60% of screen).
All images lazy-loaded with blur-up placeholder; no layout shift (CLS score < 0.1).
Offline state: app shows cached content with "You're offline" banner; no white screen of death.

9.3 Accessibility Basics
Minimum contrast ratio: 4.5:1 for body text; 3:1 for large text (WCAG AA).
All interactive elements have aria-label or accessible text.
Screen reader support for product names, prices, and CTA buttons.
No information conveyed by color alone (e.g., out-of-stock uses text label, not just greyed color).

9.4 Loading & Error States
Every async operation has a loading skeleton — never a blank white screen.
Error messages are human-readable and action-oriented: "Payment failed — try another method" not "Error 402".
Network failure: show cached state where possible; show retry button with countdown where not.

11. Security & Compliance

11.1 Authentication Security
OTP brute-force protection: 5 incorrect OTPs trigger a 1-hour lockout on that phone number.
Rate limiting: 10 auth requests per IP per minute; 5 OTP sends per phone per hour.
JWTs signed with RS256 (asymmetric); signing keys rotated quarterly.
Refresh tokens stored server-side hash (not the raw token); one-time use with rotation.

11.2 Payment Security
Razorpay webhook HMAC-SHA256 signature verified at middleware level before any handler executes.
No payment card data stored or logged — PCI DSS compliance delegated to Razorpay.
Payment amount validated server-side against order total — client-side amount ignored.
All refunds initiated via admin action only; automated refund requires admin-level JWT.

11.3 API Security
All endpoints behind HTTPS; HTTP redirects to HTTPS at Nginx/Cloudflare level.
Input validation via Pydantic on all request bodies — no raw string interpolation in queries.
SQL queries via SQLAlchemy ORM — parameterized by default; no raw SQL in business logic.
CORS configured to allow only ZISUN frontend domains; no wildcard origins.
Sensitive environment variables (DB credentials, API keys) managed via AWS Secrets Manager — never in code or .env files in production.

11.4 Data Compliance (India DPDP Act 2023)
User data collection limited to what is strictly required for commerce (name, phone, address).
Privacy policy clearly states what data is collected, why, and for how long.
Users can request data deletion; system must support soft-delete of user PII.
Data processing agreements with all vendors (Razorpay, Shiprocket, WhatsApp) reviewed.
No user data shared with third parties for advertising without explicit consent.

15. Testing Strategy

15.1 Unit Tests
All business logic functions have unit test coverage: order state machine transitions, inventory lock/release, idempotency key handling, OTP rate limiting.
Minimum coverage target: 80% on business logic modules; 100% on payment and inventory modules.
Test framework: pytest with pytest-asyncio for async FastAPI routes.

15.2 Integration Tests
Razorpay webhook handler tested with real webhook payloads (success, failure, duplicate).
Inventory race condition tested: 100 concurrent requests for 1 unit — exactly 1 succeeds.
Order state machine tested: all valid transitions, all invalid transition rejections.
Cleanup job tested: orders expire correctly; inventory locks released; no orphaned records.

16. Monetization Strategy

16.1 Phase 1–2: Direct Margin (Own Inventory)
Product margin: ₹200–₹500 per item. ZISUN sources directly and sells at a 40–60% gross margin. This is the primary revenue model for the first 6 months.
Premium positioning: Products priced at a deliberate premium over mass-market (Meesho/Flipkart). The margin is justified by curation, content quality, and brand story.

16.2 Phase 3–4: Platform Revenue
Vendor commission: 5–15% GMV commission on vendor sales transacted through ZISUN. Commission tier based on vendor GMV volume and exclusivity agreement.
Featured placement: Vendors pay for premium placement in the content feed — editorial features, occasion bundles, homepage spotlights. Priced at ₹15,000–₹50,000 per campaign.
AI Styling Services (Phase 4): Subscription-based personal styling service powered by the recommendation engine. Target: ₹299–₹499/month for personalized weekly outfit curation.

17. Future Roadmap (Year 2+)

17.1 AI & Personalization
Behavioral style fingerprinting: A per-user embedding trained on scroll patterns, dwell time, add-to-cart signals, and purchase history. Used to rank the content feed in real time.
Predictive restocking: Demand forecasting model predicts which SKUs will sell out based on content virality signals; triggers vendor purchase orders automatically.
AI Stylist Agent: Conversational agent (WhatsApp / in-app) that accepts a photo or occasion description and returns a complete styled look using catalog inventory, with one-tap checkout.

17.2 Experience Innovation
AR try-on: Garment diffusion model renders products onto user-uploaded photo or live camera. Reduces return rates and increases conversion on apparel.
Shoppable video (native): Product overlay rendered directly on video frames using computer vision. Users tap a product in a reel mid-play; bottom sheet opens without pausing video.
Social commerce graph: "3 people you follow bought this" surface in the feed. Requires opt-in social graph feature; privacy-first by design.

17.3 Platform & Infrastructure
Kafka-based streaming backbone: Replace Redis pub/sub with Kafka for durable, replayable event streaming. Enables real-time analytics, ML feature pipelines, and event sourcing.
Multi-region deployment: Active-active setup across Mumbai + Singapore for latency and resilience. Data partitioned by home_region.
Vendor self-service portal: Vendors upload products, set pricing, view analytics, and manage inventory without ZISUN ops involvement.

Strategic Vision  By Year 2, ZISUN transitions from a curated boutique to an AI-powered style discovery platform where the data flywheel — behavioral signals feeding model training feeding better personalization driving more engagement — becomes the primary competitive moat. Every product interaction from Day 1 is an investment in this flywheel. Instrument everything.
